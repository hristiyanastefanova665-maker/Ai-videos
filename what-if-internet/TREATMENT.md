# WHAT IF THE INTERNET DISAPPEARED FOREVER? — treatment

**Format:** 1080×1920, 30 fps, ~77 s. Bulgarian narration supplied by the user (`assets/StoryAudio.mp3`, 73.3 s); burned-in Bulgarian subtitles plus `.srt`.
**Look:** follows the user's reference: one stylised low-poly 3D city on a river, seen from one elevated camera, soft sun shadows, small "time without internet" counter top-left, large centred stage numbers at each jump, white serif captions. Closest library style: Low-poly Isometric Island (`lowpoly-island`) — used only for technique.

## Three candidate structures
1. **Fixed camera, time-lapse.** The camera never moves; only the world changes. Clear before/after comparison, but the first 10 s lacks energy and won't stop a scroll.
2. **Many locations.** Cut between newsroom, airport, bank and street. Rich, but breaks the "one continuous world" rule and dilutes the comparison.
3. **One world, one breathing camera (chosen).** A single city that stays recognisable — plaza, avenue, red bridge, downtown, radio mast — with a slow continuous camera that leans into whichever part of the city the line is about (phones in the plaza, the jammed avenue, the bank queue, the radio mast), then pulls back wider as the time spans grow, ending on the widest golden shot of the forest that swallowed it.
